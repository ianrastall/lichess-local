How this score works

Each level has a fixed reference value on this app’s own scale. These values are not calibrated human Elo. The score and uncertainty track your results here, separately for each variant and time-control group. A question mark means the score is provisional. Practice games do not change it.

About Lichess Local

Unofficial offline app for playing against a computer. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Lichess.

Built with Chessground, chessops, shakmaty, Fairy-Stockfish, and Lichess opening names and artwork. Game data and progress stay on this computer.
